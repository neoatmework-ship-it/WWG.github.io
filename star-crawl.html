A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away... It is a period of creative chaos. Rebel developers, striking from hidden repositories, have won their first victory against the evil Empire of Boring Websites. During the battle, rebel spies managed to steal secret plans to the Empire's ultimate weapon, the DEATH TEMPLATE, a static page with enough dullness to destroy an entire portfolio. Pursued by the Empire's sinister agents, Princess WWG races home aboard her starship, custodian of the stolen plans that can save her people and restore creativity to the galaxy...
